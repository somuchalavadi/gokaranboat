# AI visibility check

Once a month, ask each prompt below in **ChatGPT (with search)**, **Perplexity**, **Claude (with web search)**, **Gemini** and **Google (AI Overview)**. Use a fresh chat or a private window each time, and don't mention the business name unless the prompt does.

For each answer, record whether Rudra Gokarna Adventures is **mentioned**, whether **www.therudraadventure.com is cited or linked**, and whether the facts are **right** (pick-up at Gokarna Beach, 2–4 hour trips, the four packages, the phone number). A wrong fact is worth fixing at the source: usually the matching page's answer summary or key facts in `data/`.

## Prompts

### Discovery (no brand)

1. What are the best boat trips in Gokarna?
2. Where can I go dolphin watching in Gokarna?
3. Is there kayaking in Gokarna? Who runs it?
4. Can I go snorkelling in Gokarna?
5. Are there mangrove river trips near Gokarna?
6. Where can I see bioluminescence near Gokarna?
7. How do I book a private boat for a family or a couple in Gokarna?
8. What can I do on the water in Gokarna besides the beaches?

### Planning

9. How much does a boat ride cost in Gokarna?
10. What is the best time for dolphin watching in Gokarna?
11. Where do boat trips in Gokarna start from?
12. Is a Gokarna boat trip safe for children or non-swimmers?

### Brand

13. What is Rudra Gokarna Adventures?
14. What packages does Rudra Gokarna Adventures offer, and how long are they?
15. How do I contact or book Rudra Gokarna Adventures?
